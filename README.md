# 📊 Retail Sales & Customer Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes an e-commerce retail dataset of **3,000+ sales transactions** to uncover revenue trends, top-performing product categories, seasonal demand variations, and customer payment preferences. The primary objective was to clean raw operational data and translate it into an executive-ready, interactive Excel dashboard for business decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data Cleaning, Data Transformation, Pivot Tables, Dynamic Charts, Conditional Formatting.
* **Excel Functions & Features:** Power Query/Data Validation, `XLOOKUP`, `INDEX/MATCH`, `GO TO SPECIAL` (Data Imputation), Advanced Filtering.

---

## ❓ Core Business Questions Answered
1. **Total Revenue:** What is the overall Total Purchase Amount (USD)?
2. **Customer Experience:** What is the Average Review Rating across all orders?
3. **Customer Reach:** What is the total count of unique customers?
4. **Product Demand:** What are the Top 5 most purchased items? *(Visualized via Chart)*
5. **Demographics:** How are purchases distributed across different customer age groups? *(Visualized via Chart)*
6. **Seasonality:** Which season drives the highest sales volume and revenue? *(Visualized via Chart)*
7. **Payment Preferences:** What is the percentage breakdown of customer payment methods? *(Visualized via Chart)*

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation Pipeline
* **Handling Missing Data:** Identified null values across transaction fields using Excel's `Go To Special` functionality and applied appropriate imputation techniques.
* **Data Hygiene:** Standardized data types, removed duplicate records, and cleansed formatting inconsistencies using `Find & Replace` and text transformation methods.
* **Data Aggregation:** Built structured Pivot Tables to summarize high-volume transaction data for dynamic chart modeling.

---

## 💡 Key Dashboard Insights
* 👔 **Top Category Demand:** **Blouse** emerged as the single highest-selling product within the female customer demographic segment.
* 🍂 **Seasonal Sales Spike:** The business achieved its highest transaction volume and revenue during the **Fall season** compared to Spring, Summer, and Winter.
* 💳 **Payment Preference:** **Debit Card** is the primary transaction method, accounting for **33% of total payments**, followed by other digital and credit options.
